Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Hermes Backup is a private application used by its owner to store encrypted server backups in the owner's Google Drive account.

Google Drive access

The application uses Google Drive only for storing, reading, updating, and deleting backup files that are used with the application.

The application does not require general access to all files in the user's Google Drive.

Data stored

Server backup data is encrypted before it is uploaded to Google Drive.

Data sharing

Data accessed through Google APIs is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Data retention

Backup data is retained according to the owner's backup retention policy and may be deleted by the owner at any time.

Use of Google user data

Google user data is used only to provide the backup functionality requested by the account owner.

Contact

Questions regarding this privacy policy may be sent to the support email address listed on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
